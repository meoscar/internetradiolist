Privacy policy

This is the privacy policy for Nowplay: Radio World, the Android app with the package name com.meoscar.icrtradio. Last updated 6 September 2026.

The short version: the app has no accounts, asks for no personal details, and keeps your listening history on your phone. It shows advertising and reports crashes and anonymous usage through Google's libraries, which use your device's advertising ID. Everything else it fetches is public data about radio stations and music.

The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone audio, and does not ask for permission to. It has no account system.

Network requests the app makes on your behalf

To do its job the app talks to a few services. Each receives your IP address, as any internet request does, and no more than what is listed.

  • The radio stations themselves, when you play one. The station's server sees your IP address, as it would from any player.
  • GitHub, for the station catalogue and the "on the air" file the app downloads.
  • Wikipedia, for the artist's story, when you open the story panel. The artist's name is sent; nothing about you is.
  • Apple's iTunes Search, for album covers, titles and years. The song's name is sent; nothing about you is.
  • Google, for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and Chromecast if you use it.

Advertising

The app shows banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show ads it considers relevant. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised advertising in your phone's settings under Google › Ads. Google's own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your listening history

Every track the app hears is written to a database on your phone, with the station and the time. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The recap you can share is drawn on the phone and only leaves it when you press Share and choose where it goes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the current version is always at this address.
